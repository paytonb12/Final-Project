Theories and Concepts Connected to Games

Analyzing games through theory

Welcome! Here, I explore two popular games, Stardew Valley and The Sims 4 through concept and theory analysis. The content comes from a course I took titled, Game Culture where I learned to critically analyze games by examining their design, mechanics, and their role in contemporary culture.

The Stardew Valley page content is based on the first project of the course called, Game Report which was a short paper. In this section, you’ll find an overview of the game’s publication details, summary on how to play, how it connects to the course concepts, and an analysis of how the game connects to course the theory text.

The Sims 4 section features my Interpretation Paper, the second project paper from the course. On this page, I introduce and summarize the game, explain the theory I selected, and analyze how the game connects to that theory.

The two theories I examine are Agency as Art, developed by philosopher C. Thi Nguyen, and Jesper Juul’s Half-Real theory. Agency as Art is applied to The Sims 4, while Half-Real is used to analyze Stardew Valley

This website is made to deepen understanding of games beyond simply playing them. It focuses on analyzing and interpreting both the games themselves and the theories that connect to them, providing a deeper look at how games create meaning and player experience.
