About the author

My name is Payton, and I’m a junior double majoring in Digital Arts & Culture (DAC) and Journalism, Advertising & PR, and Media Studies (JAMS) with a concentration in advertising. The content on this website comes from a course I took called Game Culture. In this class, I wrote two papers analyzing video games of my choice by applying key theories and concepts we explored throughout the semester. I enjoy playing cozy, story-driven games, so I chose to focus on two of my favorites: Stardew Valley and The Sims 4. While I’m still pretty new to Stardew Valley, I’ve really enjoyed my experience so far. I’ve been a fan of The Sims series for awhile, having played both The Sims 3 and The Sims 4. My interest in video games has continued to grow, and I’m excited to explore and play more games in the future. I really enjoyed creating this website about an interest of mine while learning how to code.
